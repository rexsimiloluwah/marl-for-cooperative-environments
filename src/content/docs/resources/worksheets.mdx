Each chapter ends with a concise digital worksheet built around the same five
activities: match concepts to applications, complete two automatically checked
calculations or table analyses, explore an interactive graph, and answer one
short trade-off question. The repeated structure makes the worksheets useful
for independent review or a short class activity. Matching and numeric items
give immediate feedback; only the final item requires a written response.

## Coordinate Worksheet

Match coordination approaches to their applications, calculate joint-action
space growth and a VDN team value, explore how the number of joint actions
changes with the number of agents, and evaluate a CTDE trade-off.

[Open the Coordinate Worksheet](/coordinate/worksheet/)

## Communicate Worksheet

Match communication mechanisms to their applications, calculate message
capacity and communication cost, explore the relationship between message
loss and performance, and evaluate a communication trade-off.

[Open the Communicate Worksheet](/communicate/worksheet/)

## Adapt Worksheet

Match partner-generalization concepts to their applications, calculate a
generalization gap and cross-play performance, explore familiar and unseen
partner results, and evaluate the specialization-generalization trade-off.

[Open the Adapt Worksheet](/adapt/worksheet/)
