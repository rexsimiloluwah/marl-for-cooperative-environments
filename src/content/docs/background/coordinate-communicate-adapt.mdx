## What the Model Leaves Open

A Dec-POMDP states the problem exactly. Agents act from local observations,
the environment responds to the joint action, and one reward comes back for
the team. It says nothing about how any agent should choose.

Three gaps are left open, and each one is a chapter.

## Coordinate

**The question.** If every agent wants the same thing, why can they still fail
to work together?

Aligned objectives do not produce compatible actions. Two agents can both
pursue the team's reward and still duplicate each other's work, or both wait
for the other to move. The reward that comes back is one number for the whole
team, so it does not say which agent's choice was the problem.

Chapter 1 is about learning individual actions whose *combination* is good,
and about the training-time information that makes that possible.

## Communicate

**The question.** What should agents tell each other when they know different
things?

Partial observability puts useful information in the wrong place. One agent
can see what another needs. Communication is the option to move it, and it
comes with its own decisions: what to send, when, to whom, and what to do when
the channel is limited or the message is lost.

Chapter 2 treats a message as an action, then works through content,
constraints, learned protocols, and the failures that look alike from the
outside.

## Adapt

**The question.** Did the agent learn how to cooperate, or did it learn one
particular partner?

A team trained together develops conventions. Replace one member and the
conventions no longer hold, even though the task, the environment and the
reward are unchanged. High performance with a familiar partner is weak
evidence of an ability to cooperate.

Chapter 3 separates task competence from co-adaptation, and builds the
evaluation needed to tell them apart.

## How the Three Fit Together

They are cumulative rather than alternative. Coordination is needed before
communication has anything to improve, and both are what an agent has to carry
into a team it has not met.

<Callout variant="takeaway">
The three capabilities are diagnostic as well as instructional. A team that
fails while every agent is well informed has a coordination problem. A team
that fails because one agent lacks what another can see has a communication
problem. A team that succeeds in training and fails on a new partner has an
adaptation problem.
</Callout>

## Coordinate, Communicate, Adapt Summary

- A **Dec-POMDP** states the cooperative problem but does not say how to act
  in it. The three chapters that follow do.
- **Coordinate**: make individual actions combine well, under a shared reward
  that does not identify individual contributions.
- **Communicate**: move information to the agent whose decision it would
  change, subject to what the channel allows.
- **Adapt**: cooperate with partners that were not part of training, and
  evaluate that claim honestly.
